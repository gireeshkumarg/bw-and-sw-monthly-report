Growing

Sept 2026: search is up month on month

September clicks rose 31% (Aug 1.99k to Sep 2.61k) and impressions 23% to 217k. Average position improved from 9.7 to 8.3.

Connection: moves with the indexing gains in brands, professionals and projects and with the agency meta and title work approved in September. Cause is not isolated.

Problem

GA4 traffic is mostly bots

Iran shows 104,573 active users in 28 days with zero engaged sessions, and it is still running in early October.

Why it matters: bots inflate GA4 users and hide real performance, so the evidence pack went to IT.

Mixed

Indexation: 5 of 7 verticals up, dealers down

Brands +51%, professionals +26%, products +3%. Dealers fell about 15% (~33K to 28K), so total indexed is down about 5%. Products are still only 22.6% indexed.

Why: dealers carry the net decline, tied to the canonical issue and thin templated content. The product work in October targets the 67.5K product URLs not indexed.

New site

StudioWorld: 12 of 18 pages indexed

Indexed within days of going live in mid-Sep, with 34 clicks and 280 impressions by 3 Oct.

Why: follows the StudioWorld audit and AI Visualizer cleanup, both done in September.

Crawling

Googlebot crawl up 30% a day on BuildingWorld

About 17.3K requests a day in Sep against 13.4K in Aug. StudioWorld is crawled steadily at 230 to 300 requests a week.

Connection: crawl is the first step to indexing, and Sep indexing rose in brands, professionals and projects.
